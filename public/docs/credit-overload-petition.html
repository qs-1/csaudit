Petition for Credit Overload

FCC Information, Thu, Nov 19, 2026

  • The petition form (Real Registration Spring 2027) is active from 20 November 2026 (9AM) to 4 December 2026 (5PM), 9AM–5PM on working days.
  • You can submit a petition even with an active hold on your account. All holds must be cleared before you can proceed with course registration.
  • The AAC team needs at least one working day to decide, sometimes more. Decisions are emailed to your official FCCU address only.
  • Once approved, forward the approval email to the Academic Services Office (academicoffice@fccollege.edu.pk), Noor Mohsin (noormohsin@fccollege.edu.pk) and Areej Nadeem (areejnadeem@fccollege.edu.pk), and mention the course code(s).
  • You are responsible for making sure the courses have seats and do not clash with your timetable.

Before applying

  • Regular term (Fall/Spring): overload can be up to 20 credits, or 21 (need based) with a lab course.
  • Summer term: overload can be up to 9 credits, or 10 (need based) with a lab course.
  • CGPA: policy requires 3.0 or above. In a few cases applications below 3.0 are accepted to help students graduate on time (semesters 7 and 8, and summers after semester 7). Minimum recommended is 2.5; below that may not be accepted.
  • Computer Science majors can register up to 19 credits in a regular semester. There are 5 core courses of 4 credits to be taken in sequence by all students (around 80 credits in 5 semesters), so the credit requirement can be completed in 8 regular semesters.
  • Exemptions are made in rare cases only. AAC may dismiss an application if its information is found to be false.

Policies

  • 6-year degree tenure: graduation requirements must be completed within 6 years, including terms withdrawn.
  • 8-regular-semester policy (HEC): all students must take 8 regular (Spring and Fall) semesters to graduate. Summer and Winter terms are optional and should only be used to graduate on time, not early. FCC recognizes degrees once all requirements are complete, but HEC will not attest degrees with fewer than 8 regular semesters.
